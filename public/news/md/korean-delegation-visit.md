# BK21 BigOCEAN Research Group from Jeju National University Visits SRE² Group and SBTÜ

On September 3, 2026, **Sivas University of Science and Technology (SBTÜ)** and the **Sustainability and Renewable Energy Research Group (SRE² Group)** hosted an international delegation from the **BK21 BigOCEAN Research Group** of **Jeju National University**, Republic of Korea.

The high-level academic visit aimed to explore mutual scientific projects, strengthen international institutional cooperation, and establish joint bilateral research programs between Turkey and South Korea.

![BK21 BigOCEAN Research Group Delegation from Jeju National University at SBTÜ](/news/img/korean-delegation-visit.jpg)

## Research Leadership and Coordination

The visit was organized and coordinated by **Dr. Qazi Muhammad Saqib**, Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering and Director of **SRE² Group**, who also serves as a guest researcher at the Ocean Integrated Smart Engineering (OISE) Lab at Jeju National University. 

The delegation was supported under the **Brain Korea 21 (BK21 Four)** national flagship program by the South Korean Ministry of Education, dedicated to cultivating top-tier graduate researchers, fostering breakthrough innovation, and advancing global academic alliances.

![Commemorative Delegation Gathering at Sivas University of Science and Technology](/news/img/korean-delegation-meeting1.jpeg)

## Digital Twins, Metaverse Platforms and Smart Sensors

During the bilateral sessions held at the SBTÜ Senate Hall, researchers and faculty members evaluated strategic collaboration areas:

- **BigOCEAN Metarence Platform Demonstration:** The visiting Korean delegation showcased their advanced *BigOCEAN Metarence* digital twin platform, demonstrating how physical sensor data streams integrate seamlessly into immersive virtual environments and interactive digital twins.
- **Flexible and Wearable Electronics Seminar:** A dedicated technical seminar was conducted on *"Flexible, Wearable and Printable Electronics: Designing Architectures for Autonomous Smart Sensors in Harsh Environments"*, highlighting SRE² Group's core competencies in self-powered nanogenerators (TENG/PENG), tactile e-skin, and energy-harvesting electronic architectures.
- **Metaverse and Edge AI Integration:** Cross-disciplinary synergy between SRE² Group's sensory hardware and BigOCEAN's metaverse data infrastructure for real-time telemetry, robotics, and environmental sensing.

![Seminar on Flexible and Wearable Electronics for Autonomous Smart Sensing](/news/img/korean-delegation-seminar.jpg)

## Bilateral Research Programs and Institutional MoU

The discussions also established practical frameworks for long-term collaboration:
1. **Bilateral Research Proposals:** Formulating joint project submissions under the **TÜBİTAK – National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)** bilateral cooperation program.
2. **Academic and Researcher Exchange:** Creating bilateral exchange pathways for graduate students, postdocs, and faculty between SBTÜ and Jeju National University.
3. **Joint High-Impact Publications:** Collaborative authorship in international journals focusing on self-powered sensors, digital twins, and sustainable electronic systems.
4. **Institutional Memorandum of Understanding (MoU):** Advancing a formal MoU between SBTÜ and Jeju National University to provide institutional backing for ongoing and future collaborative projects.

This visit reflects SRE² Group's and SBTÜ's commitment to internationalization, cutting-edge technological development, and impactful global research partnerships.
