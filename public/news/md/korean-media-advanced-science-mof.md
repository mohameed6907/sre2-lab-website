# Korean Press Feature: 'Advanced Science' Highlights MOF Triboelectric Charge Control

![Veritas Alpha Korean Press Coverage](/news/img/news-veritas-advanced-science-2024.png)

> **Press Feature**: Originally published by South Korean national educational daily *Veritas Alpha (베리타스알파)* on October 2, 2024. [View Original Article](https://www.veritas-a.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=522211).

## Breakthrough in Maximizing Triboelectric Charge via Ligand Chemistry

South Korean daily *Veritas Alpha* spotlighted research published by Prof. Jinho Bae (Director of BK21 Four Big Data based Ocean Convergence Education Agency) and **co-first author Dr. Qazi Muhammad Saqib** in the prestigious international journal ***Advanced Science*** (Impact Factor: 14.3).

The study, titled **"Controlling Triboelectric Charge of Metal-Organic Frameworks by Leveraging Ligand Chemistry"**, solves fundamental charge dissipation challenges in triboelectric nanogenerators (TENGs).

---

## Key Scientific Innovations

1. **Ligand Coordination Architecture:**  
   By systematically modulating mono-, bi-, and tri-dentate ligands (2-MeIm, BDC, BTC) within metal-organic frameworks (CoCE-MOFs), the research team demonstrated atomic-level control over electron affinity and surface charge retention.

2. **Environmental Resilience:**  
   The synthesized hybrid matrix exhibited extraordinary robustness against ambient humidity and mechanical abrasion, overcoming traditional longevity limitations in wearable energy harvesters.

3. **High-Output Performance:**  
   Achieved benchmark-setting power densities capable of directly powering microelectronic sensors, medical monitors, and wireless transmission beacons without battery intervention.
