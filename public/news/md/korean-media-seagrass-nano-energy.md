## Sustainable Marine Biomass Upcycled into Functional Electronic Generators

Korean news outlet *Headline Jeju* profiled groundbreaking clean energy research led by **first author Dr. Qazi Muhammad Saqib** and Prof. Jinho Bae, demonstrating for the first time that invasive marine seagrass (*Zostera marina*) from Jeju coastal waters can be harnessed to produce electrical power.

The research was published in ***Nano Energy*** (Impact Factor: 16.8), one of the world's most prestigious energy journals.

---

## Innovation Summary

1. **Natural Tribopositive Functionalization:**  
   Extracted pure biopolymeric matrices from dried seagrass leaves to formulate a high-efficiency spray-coatable tribopositive slurry.

2. **Scalable Eco-Friendly Spray Fabrication:**  
   Allowed seamless deposition on flexible substrates, curved surfaces, and architectural panels without requiring toxic chemical solvents.

3. **Marine Ecological Integration:**  
   Combines carbon-neutral environmental protection with blue-energy harvesting, powering autonomous coastal sensors directly from ambient wave and wind vibrations.
