# Jeju Domin Ilbo: Prof. Bae Jin-ho & SRE² Researchers Spotlighted in Top Global Journals

![Jeju Domin Ilbo Korean Press Coverage](/news/img/news-jejudomin-marine-energy-2022.png)

> **Press Feature**: Reported by *Jeju Domin Ilbo (제주도민일보)* on May 18, 2022. [View Original Article](https://www.jejudomin.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=306689).

## National Media Highlights Sustained Research Excellence in World-Leading Journals

*Jeju Domin Ilbo* profiled the international research alliances and consistent top-tier publications produced by Prof. Jinho Bae and Dr. Saqib's research group in world-renowned journals including ***Advanced Functional Materials*** and ***Nano Energy***.

The feature details the laboratory's focus on blue energy technologies, self-powered ocean telemetry platforms, and high-performance hybrid energy materials designed to support carbon neutrality and maritime digital transformation.

---

## Collaborative Alliances & Impact

- Joint research execution between the Department of Ocean System Engineering, Ulsan University, and international partnering institutions.
- Multi-institutional funding support from the Ministry of Science and ICT, BK21 Stage 4, and the Korea Maritime Institute.
- Deployment of self-charging marine buoy sensor platforms continuously harvesting kinetic energy from ocean waves.
